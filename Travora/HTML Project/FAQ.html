Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers!

Discover everything you need to know before visiting Jordan

Do I need a visa to visit Jordan?

Most travelers can obtain a visa on arrival at the airport or border.
However, we recommend checking your country’s specific requirements before traveling.

What is the best time to visit Jordan?

he best time to visit Jordan is during spring (March–May) or autumn (September–November) when the weather is pleasant for sightseeing.

What are the must-visit places in Jordan?

Petra, the Dead Sea, Wadi Rum, Jerash, and Aqaba are among the top destinations you shouldn’t miss.

What should I wear in Jordan?

Dress modestly, especially when visiting religious or rural areas. Light, breathable clothing is recommended in summer

What kind of food can I expect in Jordan?

Jordanian cuisine is rich, flavorful, and diverse. Popular dishes include mansaf (the national dish made with lamb, rice, and yogurt sauce),falafel, hummus, and kebabs. You’ll also find plenty of delicious street food and sweet treats like knafeh and baklava. Most restaurants offer vegetarian options too.

Can I use credit cards in Jordan?

Credit cards are widely accepted in cities and tourist areas. However, it’s good to carry some cash for small shops or remote places.
