About us

with us, you are always in for a pleasant surprise

We are a group of enthusiastic students who created this website with the aim of promoting tourism in Jordan.
Our goal is to highlight the beauty and richness of Jordan in all its forms - from recreational and medical tourism to historical and religious attractions.

Our website reflects our love for our country and our desire to support its tourism industry by providing useful, organized and up-to-date content. Whether you're visiting Petra, the Dead Sea, or looking for a unique cultural experience, we're here to help guide your journey.

We believe that Jordan has so much to offer, and through this platform, we want to make it easier for tourists to explore the country and find the information they need to plan their trips.
We can ensure reliability, low cost fares and most important, with safety and comfort in mind
